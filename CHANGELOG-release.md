# SpellCoda Forever 0.60

- Combat: buffs that can no longer be read in combat are now kept per target, your shapeshift form stays counted, and buffs you gain or lose during combat are taken out of your character stats correctly.
- New option under Overlay: "Disable in combat" hides the action bar numbers while you fight.
- New option under Setup (Loadout): "Ignore buffs in combat" leaves buffs out of the calculation while you fight, except shapeshift forms.
- Talents you have placed but not yet applied are now previewed correctly.
- Haste now shortens the global cooldown, and spells that deal damage over time can no longer crit.
- Effects that increase critical healing are now counted.
- Calculator mode: spell tooltips show before, difference and after side by side. The item planner updates as soon as item data has loaded.
- Spell and item data updated to the current Forever client build (1.60.1.70450), including mage changes and threat values.
