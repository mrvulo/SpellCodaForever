# SpellCoda Forever 0.53

Built on SpellCoda 0.12 by jezzi23, which added its own Forever support.

- New: spell and item data generated from the Forever client (1.60.1.70291): new Forever spells are calculated (e.g. Slam, Seal of Fury, Dark Sacrifice) and every trainer spell has its training cost
- New: item stats are computed from the game's own formulas
- New: the SpellCoda window can be scaled with the grip in its bottom right corner
- Fixed: Mana Burn, feral shapeshift weapon damage, spells inside macros, buffs that alias auto attack
- Changed: updated LibDBIcon
- Changed: translation files cleaned up (unused entries removed, new item hint translated)
- New: a warning when the original SpellCoda is enabled at the same time (both share windows and commands)
- Kept: Blizzard-style window, spellbook page, icon, translations and your settings
