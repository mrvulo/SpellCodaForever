# SpellCoda Forever 0.56

- New: spellbook page prices include the 10% trainer discount from Honored reputation with a trainer town; hover the total to see each town, your standing there and the base price
- New: spellbook page section "Missing requirement" for spells whose previous rank you have not learned yet
- New: search box and settings menu on the spellbook page; right click a spell to ignore it or all its ranks, show or reset ignored spells from the settings menu
- New: items missing from the game data (e.g. some random-suffix greens like "Bandit Cloak of the Bear") are now evaluated from their own stats instead of showing "Item missing from SpellCoda dataset"
- Changed: cleaner folder structure (Core, Calc, UI, Data, Locales, Libs, Media); your settings carry over
